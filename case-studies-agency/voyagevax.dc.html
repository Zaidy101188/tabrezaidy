VoyageVax: a multi-location travel medicine clinic site

A site structured around how people actually plan a trip: where they're going, what they need, and where to get it.

  • IA
  • Multi-location
  • Booking flow
Homepage — booking & trust badges

A travel vaccination clinic with several B.C. locations needed a site that could handle destination-specific advice, vaccine information, and location booking without turning into a maze. I built dedicated destination pages, a full vaccine reference library, and a page per clinic location, all cross-linked and sharing one design system and booking flow.

A visitor can start from a destination or a specific vaccine and land in the same place — check what's needed, then book at their nearest location.
