Product cards that turned health articles into a sales channel

A contextual product card embedded in editorial content, connecting condition research to the medication that treats it.

  • Component design
  • Content strategy
  • CRO
Article page — embedded product card

Editorial traffic was large and completely unmonetised — people researching a condition never met the medication that treated it. I designed a contextual product card that sits inside the article body: plain drug name, strength, price, and a single honest action, placed only once per article so it never competes with itself.

The pattern opened a mid-funnel touchpoint that hadn't existed before, and now generates incremental revenue across the whole pharmacy portfolio.
