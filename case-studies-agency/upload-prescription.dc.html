Upload Prescription: removing the step that ended the order

A guided upload flow replacing the dead end customers hit when they had a valid paper prescription and no way to hand it over.

  • UX research
  • User flows
  • Prototyping
  • QA
Guided upload flow — screens

Customers with a valid paper prescription had no obvious way to hand it over, so they abandoned the cart and phoned the call centre — or didn't come back at all. I mapped the full journey from drug page to fulfilment, interviewed support staff about the calls they fielded daily, and rebuilt the handoff as a single guided upload with three visible stages: upload, verification, confirmation.

Carried it from research through high-fidelity prototypes, developer handoff, and QA on live traffic.
