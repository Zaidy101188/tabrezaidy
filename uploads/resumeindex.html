02 · OngoingDesign systems

One Figma system, ten brands, one set of decisions

Ten client brands meant ten versions of every button, form, and drug card — and ten chances to get an accessibility detail wrong. I built a themeable component library in Figma where structure and behaviour are shared and only colour, type, and logo change per brand.

Design time per project dropped, handoff stopped being a translation exercise, and accessibility fixes now ship once instead of ten times.

03 · 2018—2024Platform redesign

Replatforming ten pharmacy storefronts off legacy .NET

A portfolio of ageing .NET sites was slow, inconsistent, and hard to trust — a real problem when someone is deciding whether to send you their medical details. I led the end-to-end redesign onto WordPress: restructured the drug catalogue and search so people could find a medication by brand, generic, or condition, rebuilt checkout around visible trust signals, and brought every template to WCAG 2.1 AA.

Ran it as designer and project manager across concurrent client timelines, with sprint planning, design reviews, and retrospectives alongside developers and marketers.

04 · 2022Growth design

Product cards that turned health articles into a sales channel

Editorial traffic was large and completely unmonetised — people researching a condition never met the medication that treated it. I designed a contextual product card that sits inside the article body, matching the tone of the content rather than interrupting it: plain drug name, strength, price, and a single honest action.

The pattern opened a mid-funnel touchpoint that hadn't existed before and now generates incremental revenue across the whole pharmacy portfolio.

05 · 2021Feature design

Upload Prescription: removing the step that ended the order

Customers with a valid paper prescription had no obvious way to hand it over, so they abandoned the cart and phoned the call centre — or didn't come back at all. I mapped the full journey from drug page to fulfilment, interviewed support staff about the calls they fielded daily, and rebuilt the handoff as a single guided upload with clear file guidance, progress feedback, and a plain explanation of what happens next.

Carried it from research and flow mapping through high-fidelity prototypes, developer handoff, and QA on live traffic.

Practice

Design that has to earn its keep

I've spent eight years in a corner of e-commerce where the stakes are unusually personal. Someone ordering medication is managing a condition, a budget, and a fair amount of doubt about whether the site in front of them is legitimate. Clarity isn't an aesthetic preference in that context — it's the product.

My work runs the full width of the process: interviews and heuristic evaluations to find the friction, information architecture and flows to remove it, high-fidelity prototypes to prove it, and a maintained design system so the fix holds. I sit close to developers and marketers throughout, because a design that can't be built on schedule isn't a design yet.

Alongside product work I design enterprise publications — white papers, marketing collateral, brand assets — which keeps the typographic muscle strong and pays off directly in editorial and content-led commerce.
