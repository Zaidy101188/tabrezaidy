VoyageVax: a multi-location travel medicine clinic site

A site structured around how people actually plan a trip: where they're going, what they need, and where to get it.

  • IA
  • Multi-location

  • Design system
View in Figma / Adobe XD ↗

The problem

A travel vaccination clinic with several B.C. locations needed a site that could handle destination-specific travel advice, individual vaccine information, and location booking, without turning into a maze of near-identical pages.

The approach

I built out dedicated destination pages (Africa, the Caribbean and Central America, Hajj, the Indian subcontinent, Mexico, the Middle East, South America, and Southeast Asia), a full vaccine reference library covering conditions from yellow fever to typhoid, and a page per clinic location.

Every page shares one design system and the same booking call to action, so a visitor can land on a destination page, check the vaccines it needs, and book at their nearest location without leaving the pattern they started in.

Destination and vaccine pages cross-link both ways, a destination page lists the vaccines it typically requires, and each vaccine page lists the destinations where it matters, so a visitor who starts from either direction (planning a trip, or already worried about one specific vaccine) ends up in the same place. Location pages stay deliberately simple: hours, address, and a booking button, since by the time someone reaches a location page they've already decided what they need.

The outcome

30+ pages Destinations, vaccines & locations

A large content footprint that still reads as one clear path, structured around the trip a visitor is actually planning.

Adding a new destination or a new clinic location means filling in the existing template rather than designing a new page from scratch.
