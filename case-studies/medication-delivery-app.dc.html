Overview

A ten-screen prototype for a medication delivery app, built in Adobe XD as a clickable concept covering the core loop: search for a medication, place an order, track the delivery, and request a refill.

The scope stayed deliberately tight, enough screens to walk the full journey end to end, without padding it out with states nobody would click through in a review.

Each screen was linked to the next through XD's prototype mode, so a reviewer could click search, order, and track in sequence exactly as a real user would, rather than jumping between disconnected static comps.
