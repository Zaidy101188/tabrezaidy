About

I came up through publication design before product design, and it still shows.

Someone ordering medication is managing a condition, a budget, and doubt about whether the site in front of them is legitimate. Clarity isn't a preference in that context — it's the product. That's the lens I bring to every flow, from a checkout step to a comparison widget.

The work runs the full width of the process: interviews and heuristic evaluations to find the friction, information architecture and flows to remove it, high-fidelity prototypes to prove it, and a maintained design system so the fix holds. I sit close to developers and marketers throughout, because a design that can't be built on schedule isn't a design yet.

Before True North, I trained in web design at Arena Animation and started out designing enterprise white papers — twelve-plus titles for security, data centre, and networking clients — which is where the typographic habits came from.
